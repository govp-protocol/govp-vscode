# Code of conduct

Project participation must be professional, respectful and safe. Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information and abusive conduct are not accepted in issues, reviews, discussions or other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately to research@gemacode.org. Reports will be handled proportionately and with confidentiality where reasonably possible.
