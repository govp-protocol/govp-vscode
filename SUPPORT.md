# Support and lifecycle

## Getting help

- Product questions and reproducible defects: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/govp-protocol/govp-vscode/issues)
- Sensitive security reports: follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)
- Privacy or legal contact: research@gemacode.org

Before reporting a defect, run `GOVP: Show local status`, record the extension and VS Code versions, and remove secrets and personal data from logs or examples.

## Scope

Support covers installation from the official Marketplace publisher, local receipt creation and verification, schemas shipped with the extension, and provider-bound MCP integration. It does not cover arbitrary third-party MCP servers, modified builds, compromised hosts, project-specific legal compliance or the truth of a manually declared event.

Only the release lines listed in `SECURITY.md` receive fixes. A maintained release receives security updates and fixes for reproducible defects at the maintainers' discretion. There is no guaranteed response or restoration time unless a separate written service agreement says otherwise.

## Deprecation

Breaking changes, removed settings and end-of-support dates will be announced in `CHANGELOG.md` and the repository. Security-sensitive functionality may be disabled sooner when continued operation would expose users to material risk.
